Our Story

Built on purpose. Driven by impact.

Property Clinic Solutions was founded to make property and finance simple, accessible, and stress free. Our qualified specialists provide independent guidance and practical solutions built around each client's situation, not a standard template.

We support homeowners, landlords, investors, and tenants across mortgages, protection, and property education. Every recommendation is built on transparency, professionalism, and a commitment to doing what is right for the client.

Our mission is straightforward: help individuals, families, and businesses achieve their property goals with clarity, confidence, and long-term support. We are not here for one transaction. We are here to build lasting relationships.

How We Work

Getting a mortgage with us.

Most of the work is ours. Here is all it takes from you.

Step 1

Tell us about you

Answer a few quick questions, or book a free call. We learn your situation, your goals, and anything that has tripped you up before. It takes about two minutes to start.

Step 2

We find your match

We search the whole market, 90+ lenders, and bring you the options that genuinely fit, with the reasoning and the trade-offs set out clearly.

Step 3

We guide you to completion

Application, paperwork, lender liaison, and progress chasing, all handled by us. Then we set a reminder to review your rate before your next renewal.

How We Work

Arranging your protection.

A separate process, because cover is not an add-on to a mortgage.

Step 1

Protection review

A conversation about your income, your debts, your dependants, and any cover you already hold through work or elsewhere.

Step 2

Needs analysis

We work out the gap between what you have and what your household would actually need, and what it is realistic to spend closing it.

Step 3

Recommendation

We compare the whole UK protection market and recommend specific policies, with the cost and the exclusions of each set out clearly.

Step 4

Setup and annual review

We handle the application and the underwriting, then review the cover every year so it keeps pace with your life.

Our Fees

No upfront cost. No surprises.

The initial conversation is always free. There is no charge to find out whether we can help and no obligation to proceed.

If you choose to go ahead, any fee is agreed and explained in writing before we do the work, and most of our advice is paid for by the lender or insurer on completion, not by you. You will always know exactly what you are paying and why before you commit to anything.

Common Questions

What to expect, answered straight.

How long does it take to get started?

About two minutes. Answer a few questions through our Get Started form, or book a free call at a time that suits you. We respond to every enquiry within one working day.

Do I have to pay anything to talk to you?

No. The initial conversation is free and there is no obligation. We only charge a fee, where one applies, once your case completes, and we agree it with you in writing first.

Will this affect my credit score?

No. Getting started and our initial research do not affect your credit score. A full application later is a hard search, and we sequence everything to avoid unnecessary checks.

What if I have been refused before?

Tell us what happened. We work with specialist lenders who consider self-employment, adverse credit, complex income, and unusual property. Often we can help where others could not.

Do you only do mortgages?

No. We advise on mortgages and on protection: life cover, income protection, critical illness, and buildings and contents, so your home and your income are both looked after.
