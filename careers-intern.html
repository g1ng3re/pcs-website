The Role

An internship should be more than watching.

Most internships in financial services hand you a desk and a mailbox to tidy. Ours does not work that way. We are a small independent brokerage, which means the person who joins us sits next to the work rather than beside it, and picks up the parts of the job that most people only meet years later.

You will see a case from the first enquiry through to completion: how a client is qualified, how lenders and insurers actually assess an application, what makes a case fall over, and what a good adviser does when it does. Along the way you will learn how a regulated firm is run, because in this industry the process is the product.

This is a hands-on role with real responsibility, and it suits someone who wants to be useful quickly. If you are considering a career in mortgages, protection or financial advice and you want to test that idea properly before committing to it, this is the right place to do it.

The Work

What you will be doing.

No two weeks are the same. The list below is the shape of the role, not a script, and you will be encouraged to get involved anywhere you can add value.

  • Handling first contact with new enquiries: taking the details, qualifying the enquiry and booking it into an adviser's diary
  • Following up leads from events, referrals and social media, and keeping the pipeline moving so nothing goes cold
  • Preparing and packaging case files, chasing documents from clients, and keeping our case system up to date
  • Researching lender criteria and protection products so an adviser goes into a meeting with the groundwork done
  • Supporting our client events and networking sessions, from the invitation list on the way in to the follow up on the way out
  • Helping with marketing: social content, newsletters and the material we use to teach clients
  • Assisting with compliance basics, including file checks, record keeping and the paperwork a regulated firm has to keep in order
  • Sitting in on client meetings with permission, then writing the notes and the actions that come out of them

You will be given ownership of tasks early. We would rather you ask a direct question than quietly guess, and nobody here expects you to know the answer in your first month.

The Person

Who we are looking for.

We are far more interested in attitude than in a property CV. No experience in mortgages or insurance is required, and we have no interest in where you went to school.

What matters is that you are comfortable talking to people you have not met, organised enough to hold several things at once, and willing to pick up the phone before you are certain what to say. A sales or customer facing background helps, because a good part of this role is conversation.

The Return

What you will gain.

A qualification path

Support towards CeMAP, the industry standard qualification for UK mortgage advisers, once you have proved yourself in the role.

Regulated experience

Practical understanding of how an FCA regulated firm operates: advice standards, record keeping and client outcomes.

Commercial skill

Lead handling, client conversation, follow up and pipeline discipline. Skills that transfer to any sales or advisory career.

A real network

Direct access to our advisers, our clients and the people who speak at our events, plus a reference that carries weight.

Several people who have worked with us have gone on to roles in property, lending and financial services. The point of this internship is to be a foundation, not a line on a CV.

Career Path

From intern to qualified adviser.

The route is earned, not automatic. Here is what it looks like when it goes well.

Step 1

Month one: learn the ground

Induction, systems, compliance basics and product knowledge. You will listen to real client conversations and start handling enquiries with an adviser alongside you.

Step 2

Month two: take ownership

You run your own enquiry follow up, prepare case files and support live cases. You are trusted with tasks that carry a deadline and a client on the other end.

Step 3

Month three: start CeMAP

If the fit is right on both sides, we support your CeMAP study: materials, structured time and someone to answer the questions the textbook does not.

Step 4

Beyond: a role that pays

CeMAP qualified opens the door to a trainee adviser position with us, or to the wider market with real experience behind you. We will be honest with you about which is the better move.

Before You Apply

Common questions, answered straight.

Do I need experience in property or finance?

No. We will teach you the industry. What we cannot teach quickly is attitude, so we care much more about whether you are organised, curious and comfortable talking to people.

Do I need CeMAP before I apply?

No. CeMAP is where this role leads, not where it starts. If you have already begun studying, say so in your application, because it tells us something useful about how you work.

Can I do this alongside university or another job?

Yes, and several people do. We ask for a minimum of 16 hours a week and we agree the pattern with you. What we need is consistency, not a particular set of days.

What does the application process look like?

You apply through the form on this page. If your application fits, we will invite you to an informal conversation, then a second meeting with the adviser you would work alongside. We aim to come back to every applicant within ten working days.

Will I be giving advice to clients?

No. Regulated advice is given by qualified advisers only. You will support the process, prepare the groundwork and speak to clients about their case, all under supervision.

What happens to my application data?

It is stored securely and used only to assess your application. We keep unsuccessful applications for six months, then delete them. You can ask us to delete yours at any time by emailing info@propertyclinicsolutions.com.
