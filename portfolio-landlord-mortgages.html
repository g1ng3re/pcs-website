Portfolio Landlords

What changes at four properties.

Most lenders treat you as a portfolio landlord once you own four or more mortgaged buy-to-let properties, counting every lender, not just one. From that point each application is assessed against your whole portfolio.

Expect to provide a schedule of every property, with its value, rent, mortgage balance and monthly payment, plus details of your experience and plans. One property with weak rental cover can affect the whole application, so we review the portfolio before anything is submitted.

Complex Buy to Let

When a buy-to-let case becomes complex.

Four or more properties

Portfolio landlord underwriting, with the whole portfolio assessed on each application.

Mixed ownership

Some properties held personally and some in a limited company, or held jointly with others.

HMOs and multi-unit blocks

Property types that only some lenders consider, alongside standard lets in the same portfolio.

Self-employed income

Company directors, contractors and sole traders whose income needs to be presented properly.

Tight rental cover

Cases where the rent does not meet a lender's standard stress test, and a different lender or structure may help.

Refinancing to grow

Releasing equity across several properties to fund the next purchase.

Common Questions

Portfolio landlord FAQs.

What is a portfolio landlord?

Most lenders define a portfolio landlord as someone with four or more mortgaged buy-to-let properties. Lenders apply stricter underwriting to portfolio cases and assess the whole portfolio on each application.

Do properties owned through a limited company count?

Usually, yes. Lenders tend to count mortgaged properties you own personally and through companies you control. We check how each lender treats your structure.

Can one weak property stop me borrowing?

It can. Lenders look at rental cover across the whole portfolio, so one property that does not stack up can affect the next application. We identify weak spots before anything is submitted.

Can I refinance several properties at once?

Yes. Depending on the lender, properties can be refinanced together or one by one. We look at which approach suits your plans and your timings.

What makes a buy-to-let mortgage complex?

Common reasons include owning four or more properties, mixing personal and limited company ownership, HMOs or multi-unit blocks, self-employed income, or rent that does not meet a lender's standard stress test.
