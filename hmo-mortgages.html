What Counts as an HMO

A house in multiple occupation, explained.

A property is usually an HMO (house in multiple occupation) when it is let to three or more tenants who form more than one household and share facilities such as a kitchen or bathroom. A household is a single person, a couple, or a family living together.

In England, a large HMO, let to five or more people from more than one household, needs a mandatory licence from the local council. Many councils also run additional licensing schemes for smaller HMOs, and some areas need planning permission for HMO use under an Article 4 direction. Lenders ask about all of this, so we check it with you before an application goes in.

Key Facts

How HMO mortgages differ from standard buy to let.

Fewer Lenders

Many high street lenders do not lend on HMOs, or limit the number of bedrooms. Specialist lenders fill the gap, and their criteria differ widely.

Landlord Experience

Some lenders want you to have let property before. Others consider first-time HMO landlords on the right case.

Valuation Basis

An HMO may be valued as a house (bricks and mortar) or on the income it produces (investment basis). The basis used can change how much you can borrow.

Licensing and Standards

Lenders typically expect the property to meet licensing, fire safety and room size standards before or soon after completion.

Common Questions

HMO mortgage FAQs.

What is an HMO mortgage?

An HMO mortgage finances a house in multiple occupation: a property let to three or more tenants who form more than one household and share facilities. Specialist lenders are usually required and the property must meet local licensing rules.

Can I get an HMO mortgage as a first-time landlord?

With some lenders, yes. The choice is narrower and criteria are stricter, so how the case is presented matters. We look for lenders who consider first-time HMO landlords on your profile.

How much deposit do I need for an HMO?

Most HMO lenders look for a deposit of at least 25%. Some accept less on the right case, and a larger deposit can open up better rates.

Do I need an HMO licence before I apply?

A large HMO must be licensed before it is let to five or more people from more than one household, and lenders will usually ask for evidence. Councils can also license smaller HMOs, so we check the local rules early.

Can I buy an HMO through a limited company?

Yes. Many HMO landlords buy through a limited company. We advise on the mortgage and work alongside your accountant, who advises on the tax position.

Are HMO mortgage rates higher than standard buy to let?

Often slightly, because fewer lenders take part. A higher rental yield can offset the difference, and we work through the numbers with you before you commit.
